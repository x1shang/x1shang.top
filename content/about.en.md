*A quiet personal space for research, creative work, and everyday life.*

This is a simple little site: no comments, likes, submission forms, or backend. The command-line search at the bottom runs entirely in your browser. Even publishing a post is as simple as dropping a file into the repository. This page, for instance, lives in `content/about.en.md`; edit that file to update it. Markdown and $\LaTeX$ are both supported.

The site's source code is on GitHub under the MIT license. Why not try building a blog of your own?

### About me

**2014–2020 · Beijing No. 2 Experimental Primary School.** I served as math class representative, class committee member for studies, class monitor, and head of the school's Young Pioneers. I also played double bass in the orchestra, eventually becoming principal bassist. So many things began there: a love of science in the classroom, a growing sensitivity to music through hours of practice, seven trips to Tibet and two to the United States, my first attempts at skiing and surfing, and a taste for poetry that led me to start a poetry club at school. Those years feel distant now, but they still shape who I am.

**2020–2023 · Branch of RDFZ.** I was the Chinese class representative and helped with class publicity. The pandemic was woven into my middle-school years. There were no great upheavals in my own life, just studying from home, filling notebook after notebook with thoughts, and discovering online, for the first time, what AI could do. Life was ordinary, and increasingly digital.

**2023–2026 · The High School Affiliated to Renmin University of China (RDFZ).** I was the member of the second cohort of the Basic Science Experimental Class. Moving from the branch school to the main school was an eye-opening experience. Clubs, class events, competitions, challenges, research projects, and study trips suddenly filled my days. My classmates each had a distinct character and pursued their own interests with dedication. Getting to know them, taking on physics competitions, and spending evenings writing lyrics for a closing-credits song for the school film festival often pushed me beyond what I thought I could do. I left with plenty of regrets, but also a clearer sense of what I wanted to pursue.

**2026–present · Shanghai Jiao Tong University.** I'm studying computer science, with the aim of doing research at the intersection of AI and physics.

**Get in touch:** [zhu_jiahe2008@sjtu.edu.cn](mailto:zhu_jiahe2008@sjtu.edu.cn).
